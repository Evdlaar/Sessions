# Session Repository

Welcome to my session repository.
Inside this repository, you can find the slides, demos, and additional materials that are part of the presentations.

As it happens, I am frequently asked to deliver the same session at multiple events. In many cases, the content of the slides and/or additional materials does not change. However, if it does, I make sure to always upload the most recent version of the session here. This can mean that the slides may contain logos from a different event than expected.

As you can see in the folder structure, each session has a seemingly random letter in brackets in front of it. These letters are less random than you might think and actually indicate the type of session. The list below shows the abbreviations and their meanings:

- LT: Lightning talk at an event/conference
- RS: Regular 50/60-minute session at an event/conference
- GL: Guest lecture at a high school/university
- FD: Full-day workshop at an event/conference

Next to the abbreviation, I have included a language code. While most session materials are available in English (EN), I sometimes present in Dutch (NL).

Wondering where you can find the materials for the Crazy Data Science sessions?
They are available on their own [GitHub repository](https://github.com/crzydatascience)
